Features that match how you actually edit

Keyboard-first, timeline-clear, no 47 panels fighting for attention.

S

Split at playhead

Razor cuts where the action is. No hunting for a buried tool.

M

Mark the moment

Flag kills, clutches, and funny deaths. Jump back from the inspector list.

Del

Ripple delete

Remove a clip and the timeline closes the gap. Stay tight.

✂

Cut all silences

Bulk-trim dead air from AI suggestions — including mid-clip silence.

◆

Highlight export

Export ±3s around every marker as a merged highlight reel.

⚡

Proxy scrubbing

Long 1080p sessions get a lightweight proxy so scrubbing stays usable.

From raw session to reel

A workflow built for ranked VODs — not wedding films.

  1. 01

    Import

    Drop your Bloodstrike / CODM recording. Luna probes duration and starts proxy + audio in the background.

  2. 02

    Mark & cut

    Play, hit M on good moments, S to split, drag edges to trim. Apply silence cuts when AI surfaces them.

  3. 03

    Export

    Full timeline export — or Highlights for a tight reel around your markers only.

“Neovim philosophy” for video — the project stays cheap in memory. Heavy work (decode, AI, export) is isolated. You edit an EDL, not a pile of decoded frames.
